# Hospitality-Revenue-Insights

**Project Overview**
AtliQ Grands, a legacy chain of five-star hotels in India with 20 years of industry experience, is currently facing a decline in market share and revenue within the luxury and business categories. This decline is attributed to aggressive competitor strategies and gaps in data-driven decision-making.

As a Data Analyst, I acted as a third-party consultant to implement Business and Data Intelligence strategies. My goal was to analyze historical data and provide actionable insights to help the revenue management team regain their competitive edge.

**Project Goals**
The primary objective was to build a comprehensive dashboard that allows stakeholders to monitor revenue trends and room occupancy. Key tasks included:

Metric Development: Creating a robust set of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) based on specific business requirements.

Dashboard Implementation: Designing a stakeholder-ready dashboard following a provided mock-up.

Strategic Insights: Identifying trends and patterns beyond the basic metrics to guide the Managing Director’s strategy.
